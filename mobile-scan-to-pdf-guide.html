How to Scan Documents with a Phone and Save as PDF | KAGZI

Turn a paper page into a shareable digital document with a careful phone-camera workflow. This guide explains lighting, framing, multi-page capture and how to bring scanned images into a PDF without assuming that every phone camera produces an official scan.

1. Prepare the page and camera

  • Place the paper on a flat, clean surface with a background that contrasts with the page.
  • Use bright, even light. Avoid shadows from your hand and reflections from glossy paper.
  • Hold the phone parallel to the page. Keep all four corners inside the frame.
  • Tap to focus on the text and wait until the image looks sharp before capturing.

Practical tip: Capture the full page first. Do not crop close to signatures, stamps, page numbers or handwritten notes.

2. Review every captured page

Open each photo and check that small print, dates, names and document numbers can be read at normal zoom. Retake blurred or tilted images. If the paper has multiple pages, capture them in the correct order and give each image a meaningful file name.

What a good scan should show

Complete edges

All page corners and important margins are visible.

Readable text

Letters and numbers are sharp, not smeared by motion.

Natural colour

Text, seals and marks remain distinguishable without extreme filters.

3. Turn scanned images into a PDF with KAGZI

  1. Open KAGZI PDF Document Studio in a modern browser.
  2. Use the image-to-PDF or add-image workflow available in the workspace to bring in the captured pages.
  3. Arrange pages in the same order as the paper document.
  4. Export or download the PDF using the available control.
  5. Open the exported file and verify page order, orientation, margins and readability.

The editor also includes a camera scanner panel on supported devices. Camera access is requested only after you choose to open the camera, and browser permission plus HTTPS are required.

4. Before sharing or submitting

  • Check that every page belongs to the same document.
  • Confirm that no page is upside down, missing or duplicated.
  • Check the receiving website's accepted format, file-size limit and page requirements.
  • Keep the paper original and an unedited copy of your images.
  • Do not upload confidential documents to a public or shared device without permission.

Frequently asked questions

Can I scan a document using my phone?

Yes. Use the phone camera in good, even light, keep the camera parallel to the page and check the captured image for sharpness and complete page edges.

Does KAGZI's PDF editor have a camera scanner?

The current PDF Document Studio includes a camera scanner panel. Camera permission is requested when you choose Open camera; HTTPS and browser/device support are required.

Will a phone photo automatically become an accepted official scan?

No. Image quality and acceptance depend on the receiving organisation's current instructions. Check its format, size, colour and page rules.

How do I combine multiple scanned pages?

Capture each page, add the images through the available image-to-PDF or document workflow, arrange them in order and verify the exported PDF.
