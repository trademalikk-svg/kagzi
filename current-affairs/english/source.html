UPSC Civil Services Main Examination 2026 written result declared

National · Urban Transport

India’s metro network reaches approximately 1,200 km

A PIB release dated 5 November stated that India’s metro network has expanded from 248 km across five cities in 2014 to approximately 1,200 km across 26 cities. More than one crore passengers use metro services every day, according to the release.

Context: Metro rail is a high-capacity urban public transport system. Network expansion is linked to urbanisation, traffic management and public transport infrastructure.

Exam relevance: Urban development, transport infrastructure and sustainable cities.

Official source: PIB — Metro network expansion, 5 Nov 2026

Economy · Fiscal Federalism

₹1,09,019 crore released to States as advance tax devolution

The Union Government released a regular tax devolution instalment of ₹1,09,019 crore to States on 5 November 2026. The instalment was normally due on 10 November but was released early to support States’ capital and welfare expenditure ahead of upcoming festivals.

Uttar Pradesh focus: The State-wise table in the PIB release lists ₹19,208 crore for Uttar Pradesh.

Exam relevance: Tax devolution, Centre–State financial relations and fiscal federalism. Tax devolution is a transfer of the Union’s shareable tax revenue to States.

Official source: PIB — Tax devolution to States, 5 Nov 2026

Rural Development · Livelihood

Joint advisory on fodder and livestock infrastructure under VB–G RAM G

The Department of Rural Development and the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying issued joint advice to States and Union Territories on strengthening fodder security, pasture development and livestock-supporting infrastructure. PIB states that the VB–G RAM G Act, 2025 provides a statutory guarantee of 125 days of wage employment for each rural household and came into force on 1 July 2026.

Key facts: The advisory identifies 22 permissible works related to fodder production and storage, pastures, animal shelters, dairy facilities and veterinary services.

Exam relevance: Rural employment, livestock economy, Gram Panchayat planning and livelihood assets.

Official source: PIB — Fodder and livestock infrastructure advisory, 5 Nov 2026

Science & Technology · Astronomy

UBC 63 identified as a system of two distinct stellar clusters

A team led by Indian astronomers identified UBC 63 not as a single open star cluster but as a system comprising UBC 63A and UBC 63B. The system is reported to be about 4,892 light-years from Earth. The analysis used stellar position and motion data from the European Space Agency’s Gaia mission.

Why it matters: Studying the structure and motion of two stellar clusters after a close encounter can improve understanding of the evolution of star clusters in the Milky Way.

Exam relevance: Open star clusters, the Milky Way, gravity and the Gaia mission.

Official source: PIB — UBC 63 double-cluster finding, 5 Nov 2026

Environment · Water Resource Management

National consultation on draft technical guidelines for watershed management

The Department of Land Resources, National Rainfed Area Authority and ICAR–NBSS&LUP held a two-day national consultation on draft technical guidelines for improved watershed management on 4–5 November. The discussion took place under the World Bank-assisted REWARD programme.

Key ideas: Scientific evidence, technology, cost-effectiveness, flexibility for local conditions and community participation. The guidelines were described as being at the draft/consultation stage, not as a final notification.

Exam relevance: Rainfed agriculture, soil and water conservation, watershed areas and participatory rural development.

Official source: PIB — Watershed management consultation, 5 Nov 2026

Environment · Biodiversity

2026 guidelines issued for India’s I-OECMs

India’s Environment Ministry issued national guidelines during CBD COP-17 to identify and report Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures (OECMs). These may be areas that are not legally notified protected areas but make an effective contribution to long-term biodiversity conservation. Examples include community-managed water bodies, sacred groves, biodiversity parks and some coastal or marine areas. I-OECM recognition does not automatically turn an area into a legally protected area or change its ownership.

Exam relevance: Biodiversity, in-situ conservation, the Kunming–Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework and the 30x30 target.

Official source: PIB — MoEFCC, 4 Nov 2026.

Energy & Economy

₹14,500 crore VGF scheme for grid-scale energy storage

The Union Cabinet approved a Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme with an outlay of ₹14,500 crore to support the development of 92 GWh of grid-scale energy storage systems. Large-scale storage can help integrate renewable energy and improve electricity grid management. VGF is financial support intended to make socially or economically useful but financially challenging projects viable.

Exam relevance: Energy storage, solar and wind integration, grid stability and public financial support.

Official source: PIB — Cabinet releases, 4 Nov 2026.

Governance & Statistics

VISTAR scheme approved for 2026–27 to 2030–31

VISTAR stands for Viksit Bharat Initiative for Statistical Transformation, Analytics & Research. It was approved as a Central Sector Scheme for 2026–27 to 2030–31. Its focus is strengthening government statistics, analytics and research systems.

Exam relevance: Quality and timeliness of official statistics, evidence-based policymaking and institutional statistical capacity.

Official source: PIB — Cabinet releases.
