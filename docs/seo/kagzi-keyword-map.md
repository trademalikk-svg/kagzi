## Implementation rules

1. Keep one primary intent per existing landing page. Do not repeat the same title and primary phrase across multiple pages.
2. Update each page's title, H1, opening paragraph, useful subheadings, image alt text and internal links to match its assigned intent naturally. Do not stuff keyword lists into visible copy.
3. Do not create near-duplicate pages for close variants such as 20KB and 50KB; cover both variants on the existing photo-compression page.
4. Keep Hindi and English pages as genuine language adaptations with reciprocal hreflang links, not duplicated blocks.
5. Put the actual working tool near the top of tool landing pages. Explain supported formats, steps, output limits and tested mobile behaviour accurately.
6. Jan Seva must remain an independent guide, not imply government affiliation or certificate issuance. Put current official source links and verification dates near the relevant service information.
7. Education pages must state the actual subject/syllabus coverage, contents, update date and preview/access details. Do not claim official affiliation, guaranteed results or complete coverage without evidence.
8. Do not add estimated search volume, ranking positions, competitor traffic or backlinks unless a connected source returns those metrics. Ahrefs competitor metrics were previously unavailable under the connected plan.
9. Do not claim the Top 5 goal has been achieved until Google Search Console confirms the actual positions.
