Split PDF vs. extract pages

Splitting usually means dividing a document into smaller files or page groups. Extracting means selecting specific pages and saving those pages as a new PDF. The right choice depends on whether you need every page divided or only a few pages copied into a separate file.

Split by range

Use a start page and end page when you need a continuous section, such as pages 5–12.

Extract selected pages

Use page selection when you need only particular pages, such as a form and its supporting page.

Steps to extract a page range in KAGZI

  1. Open KAGZI PDF Document Studio.
  2. Select the PDF file and wait for the page count to load.
  3. Find the Split PDF pages controls.
  4. Enter the first and last page numbers for the section you need.
  5. Check that the range is within the document's page count.
  6. Choose Download selected pages and open the new PDF to verify it.

Example: If the source has 24 pages and you need pages 6 through 9, enter 6 as the start and 9 as the end. Confirm that the downloaded PDF contains exactly those four pages.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Counting the cover page incorrectly. The PDF page number may differ from the printed page number.
  • Entering an end page smaller than the start page.
  • Forgetting that a scanned document may have blank or rotated pages.
  • Sharing an extracted page without the context or attachments it depends on.
  • Overwriting the only copy of the original file.

Privacy and document checks

The PDF editor describes local browser processing: the selected PDF is processed on your device rather than uploaded to KAGZI by this editor. Public libraries may load from a CDN. Before sharing an extracted file, check for personal information, hidden pages, signatures and any page that should not be disclosed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract only pages 3 to 7?

Yes. Use the Split PDF pages controls in KAGZI Document Studio, enter 3 as the start and 7 as the end, then verify the downloaded file.

Does splitting delete pages from my original PDF?

The tool creates a separate output file. Keep your source PDF as a backup.

What if my PDF has printed page numbers that differ from the viewer?

Use the page number shown by the PDF viewer and inspect the page thumbnails before exporting.

Are files uploaded during PDF splitting?

The KAGZI PDF editor processes the selected PDF locally in your browser. Check the privacy notice inside the tool before use.
