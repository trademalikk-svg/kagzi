Important passport photo note

Photo size presets apply dimensions only. They do not certify face position, background colour, image quality or acceptance by a government office, embassy or visa authority. Always check the current instructions of the organisation receiving your photo.

Privacy: Normal photo editing runs in your browser tab. AI background removal loads its model from a public CDN only after you request it. Keep your original image as a backup.

Frequently asked questions

How can I reduce photo size online?

Open Photo Studio, choose the compress-to-target-size tool, enter a target in KB and review the actual output size and quality.

Can I resize a photo without changing its proportions?

Use the resize tool and keep the aspect-ratio option enabled.

Does a passport photo preset guarantee acceptance?

No. A preset changes dimensions only. Check the receiving authority's latest photo requirements before submission.
