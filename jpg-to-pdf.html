How to convert JPG to PDF online

  1. Open the KAGZI PDF Studio.
  2. Find the Images to PDF tool.
  3. Tap Choose images and select JPG, JPEG or PNG files from your device.
  4. Check that you selected the correct images.
  5. Tap Create PDF and save the generated document.
  6. Open the PDF to check page order, readability and file size.

Tip: For forms or official submissions, check the receiving website's accepted file type, dimensions and size limit. Converting an image to PDF does not automatically make it compliant.

JPG, JPEG and PNG: what's the difference?

JPG / JPEG

These are names for the same common photo format. JPG files are often smaller because they use lossy compression.

PNG

PNG is useful for screenshots, graphics and sharp text. It can produce larger source files than JPG.

PDF

A PDF can package multiple image pages into one document, making it convenient to share or print.

When is an images-to-PDF converter useful?

  • Collect photographed handwritten notes into one PDF.
  • Combine scanned receipts or records for personal organization.
  • Package several image pages for sharing with a teacher or colleague.
  • Prepare an image-based PDF attachment where the receiving service explicitly allows it.

Image-to-PDF conversion does not turn words in a photo into selectable text. For searchable text, an OCR step may be required. If image quality is low, the resulting PDF will not automatically restore missing detail.

Before downloading: quality checklist

  • Use clear, upright source images with legible text.
  • Crop unnecessary background before conversion if needed.
  • Check the selected image order.
  • Inspect the resulting PDF on your phone or computer.
  • Keep your original images in case you need to remake the document.
