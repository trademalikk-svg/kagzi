ABOUT KAGZI

Documents, made simpler.

KAGZI is being built as an all-in-one workspace to help people work with everyday documents in one place.

Our purpose

Our goal is to bring useful document workflows together: PDF utilities, image-to-text scanning, photo preparation, office document tools, forms, document organisation and AI-assisted help.

What is available today?

The current workspace includes browser-based PDF operations, image OCR and photo resizing/conversion. These tools process selected files in your browser where indicated. Other areas are being developed and should not be treated as active services until they are visibly enabled.

AI assistance

KAGZI is designed to offer Hindi and English AI assistance for product questions and document-related guidance. AI features require a securely configured server connection and may not be available until setup is complete. AI output should be checked before relying on it.

Our approach

We aim to explain what each feature does, be clear about whether processing happens locally or on a server, and avoid claiming that a feature is available before it has been tested.
