How to reduce photo size in KB

  1. Open KAGZI Photo Studio and choose your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Open the compression section and use the target-size or quality controls available there.
  3. Download the processed image and check its actual file size in KB.
  4. Open the saved image and review its clarity before uploading it to a form.
  5. If the result looks unclear, use a larger file-size target or start again from the original image.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a photo to 20KB online?

Use the photo compression controls, choose the target if available, download the result and verify its actual size. Not every image can reach a very small target while remaining clear.

Can I reduce a signature to 20KB?

Use image compression only if the form permits the resulting file format and dimensions. Check that the signature remains legible.

Will a 50KB photo work for every exam or government form?

No. Requirements vary by authority, application and year. Read the latest instructions on the official application website.

Are my selected photo files uploaded to KAGZI?

Standard photo editing is designed to run in your browser. AI background removal may load its model from a public content delivery network when you request it. Review the Privacy Policy for details.
