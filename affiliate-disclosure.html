Affiliate Disclosure

How links may support KAGZI, and what that means for you.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. What are affiliate links?

Some websites and services operate affiliate programmes. If a website visitor follows a qualifying affiliate link and makes a purchase or completes another eligible action, the website that shared the link may receive a commission or referral fee.

2. How this may support KAGZI

If you make a purchase through an affiliate link clearly identified by KAGZI, KAGZI may receive a commission. You pay no extra charge because of this commission. The price you pay is not increased because KAGZI may receive a commission.

3. How to identify affiliate links

When affiliate links are introduced, KAGZI will identify them with a nearby “Affiliate link” label or a clear disclosure before you follow the link. Not every external link on KAGZI is an affiliate link. Links to official government portals and other ordinary references should not be treated as affiliate links unless they are specifically labelled.

4. Current status

As of the date above, no active affiliate network or advertising network is being claimed by KAGZI. No affiliate partner or commission arrangement is being represented as active here. If that status changes, this page will be updated with the relevant information.

5. Recommendations and your choice

Any future recommendation should be selected for its relevance to the topic, not solely because a commission may be available. Please review the product or service description, price, terms, and provider information before making a decision.

6. Questions

If you have a question about this disclosure, contact KAGZI at hello@kagzi.in.

For information about data handling, please also read the Privacy Policy. You can return to the KAGZI workspace or review the Terms & Conditions.
