How to make a passport-size photo

  1. Open KAGZI Photo Studio and select your JPG, PNG or WebP image in Resize & Convert.
  2. Open the Passport & Visa Photo section and choose the dimension preset required by your application.
  3. Use the resize controls to process the image. Keep the aspect ratio setting in mind; check the resulting framing rather than stretching a face or image unnaturally.
  4. If needed, use Crop & Rotate to adjust the image. Crop coordinates are measured from the top-left corner of the source image.
  5. Use the background feature only if the receiving authority permits the chosen background. AI background removal runs only after you press its button.
  6. If the portal specifies a file-size limit, use Compress to target size, enter the requested KB value and check the actual downloaded size and clarity.
  7. Open the final file and compare it with the authority's current photo checklist before uploading or printing.

Passport photo checklist before submission

  • Confirm the exact dimensions and whether the authority asks for a printed photo or a digital upload.
  • Check the required background colour, face position, head size, expression and whether glasses or head coverings are permitted.
  • Check file format, maximum file size, pixel dimensions and any minimum resolution rules.
  • Use a clear, recent photograph and avoid filters or edits that change your appearance.
  • Keep the original image and inspect the processed file at full size.
  • Do not assume that selecting a preset or changing the background makes a photo officially compliant.

Privacy and file handling

KAGZI Photo Studio states that the selected image is processed in the current browser tab and is not uploaded to KAGZI. The AI background-removal feature fetches its model and library from a public CDN only after you request it. Use a trusted device, avoid shared devices for sensitive photos and keep your original image as a backup.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a passport-size photo online?

Open Photo Studio, select an image, choose the dimension preset that matches your application, then process and download the image. Verify the complete photo requirements before submission.

Which sizes can I choose in KAGZI?

The listed starting presets are 35 × 45 mm, 2 × 2 inch, 3 × 4 cm and 25 × 35 mm. They are not universal requirements.

Will the passport-size preset guarantee acceptance?

No. It applies dimensions only. It does not verify face placement, background, image quality or any authority's acceptance rules.

Can I make the photo under 50 KB or 100 KB?

Enter the requested target in the Compress to target size tool. The actual result depends on the source image; check both the file size and visual quality after downloading.

Can I change the photo background?

The Photo Studio includes an AI background-removal option and a solid-colour background control for an AI-cut-out image. Confirm that edits and the selected background are allowed for your application.

Are photos uploaded to KAGZI?

The Photo Studio says selected images are processed in the browser tab and are not uploaded to KAGZI. The AI model and library are fetched from a public CDN after you request background removal.
