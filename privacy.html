KAGZI POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy describes the current KAGZI website and the practices identified in the website code reviewed on 6 November 2026. It will be updated when material services or data practices change.

1. Files processed in your browser

Current PDF, OCR and photo tools are designed to process selected files locally in your browser. Files are processed locally in your browser where the tool indicates this behavior. The workspace's temporary document list references files only for the current browser session; it does not currently provide account-based cloud storage. Do not use a shared device for confidential files.

2. AI assistant messages

When the AI assistant is enabled, the text you submit will be sent to KAGZI's server function and then to the configured AI provider to generate a reply. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or sensitive identity/document numbers. The AI provider may process submitted text under its own terms and privacy policy. Provider-specific retention and processing details should be reviewed before submitting information.

3. Third-party libraries and services

The website currently loads some fonts and document-processing libraries from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive standard technical request information, such as IP address, browser details and requested resource information, when your browser loads their resources. These technical resource providers are not being described as advertising or analytics partners.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

In the website code reviewed for this update, we did not identify an explicit document.cookie implementation or a cookie-consent banner in the main workspace. This does not guarantee that the hosting platform, browser, or every third-party resource uses no technical storage or similar technology. Your browser may also provide its own storage and privacy controls. KAGZI is not currently claiming that optional advertising or analytics cookies are in use.

5. Analytics

No active analytics provider or analytics integration was identified in the reviewed website code as of 6 November 2026. KAGZI is therefore not claiming that a third-party analytics service is currently active. If an analytics service is added, this policy will be updated to identify the service and explain the relevant data practices.

6. Advertising

No active advertising network or AdSense integration was identified in the reviewed website code as of 6 November 2026. This page does not mean that advertisements are currently being served. If third-party advertising is enabled later, KAGZI will update this policy with information about the provider and any related cookies or similar technologies before describing that service as active.

7. Affiliate links

KAGZI may in the future include clearly identified affiliate links. If you make a purchase through an identified affiliate link, KAGZI may receive a commission without adding any extra cost to you. Not every external link is an affiliate link. No active affiliate network or affiliate programme is being claimed as active on this website as of 6 November 2026. Read our Affiliate Disclosure for more information.

8. Information we do not currently offer to store

At present, the website does not provide a verified user account, persistent document locker, or a confirmed support-ticket database. Do not assume that files or chat messages are saved to an account or that a complaint has been formally registered.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@kagzi.in.

10. Changes

This policy may change as KAGZI features and services are added. The date above should be updated whenever this policy is revised.
