KAGZI POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy describes the KAGZI website and the data practices currently implemented in its published code. It will be updated when material services or data practices change.

1. Files processed in your browser

Current PDF, OCR and photo tools are designed to process selected files locally in your browser where indicated. The workspace's temporary document list references files only for the current browser session; it does not currently provide account-based cloud storage. Do not use a shared device for confidential files.

2. AI assistant messages

When the AI assistant is enabled, the text you submit will be sent to KAGZI's server function and then to the configured AI provider to generate a reply. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or sensitive identity/document numbers. The AI provider may process submitted text under its own terms and privacy policy. Provider-specific retention and processing details should be reviewed before submitting information.

3. Third-party libraries and services

The website loads some fonts and document-processing libraries from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive standard technical request information, such as IP address, browser details and requested resource information, when your browser loads their resources.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

KAGZI's analytics preference is stored in your browser's local storage as kagzi_analytics_consent. The Google Analytics tag is configured with analytics storage denied by default. It starts sending analytics events only if you select “Allow analytics”. If you select “Reject”, analytics remains disabled on that browser. You can clear the preference through your browser's site-data settings.

5. Analytics

On KAGZI pages that load the shared site configuration, KAGZI uses Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) with measurement ID G-B825M6CQ5X to measure page views and general website usage after consent. Depending on your settings and Google’s processing, analytics may include page URL/title, device and browser information, approximate region, interaction events and online identifiers. KAGZI does not intentionally send uploaded document files or their contents to Google Analytics through this tag. Analytics is optional: it is not started until you select “Allow analytics”. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising

No active advertising network or AdSense integration is being claimed as active on this website. If third-party advertising is enabled later, KAGZI will update this policy with information about the provider and any related technologies.

7. Affiliate links

KAGZI may in the future include clearly identified affiliate links. If you make a purchase through an identified affiliate link, KAGZI may receive a commission without adding any extra cost to you. Not every external link is an affiliate link. Read our Affiliate Disclosure for more information.

8. Information we do not currently offer to store

At present, the website does not provide a verified user account, persistent document locker, or a confirmed support-ticket database. Do not assume that files or chat messages are saved to an account or that a complaint has been formally registered.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@kagzi.in.

10. Changes

This page may change as KAGZI features and services are added. The date above should be updated whenever this policy is revised.
