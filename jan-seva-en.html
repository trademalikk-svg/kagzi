Understand Government Services and Documents

KAGZI helps citizens find plain-language information about selected central and Uttar Pradesh government services. Explore document checklists, learn what to verify before applying and continue to the relevant official portal.

Certificates and identity

Understand common requirements for income, caste, residence, birth and identity-related services. Final eligibility and documents must be checked on the official portal.

Family and welfare

Find starting points for ration-card, pension, family and social-welfare information.

Education and scholarships

Explore student support and scholarship information, and verify current dates and eligibility with the administering authority.

Farmers and rural services

Find information about selected farmer and rural programmes and their official application sources.

Employment and public services

Browse employment-related information and links to public-service resources.

Document preparation

Use checklists to prepare scans and supporting documents for online forms. Never share OTPs, passwords or full identity numbers in chat.

Important: KAGZI is an independent information service, not a government department. It does not issue, approve or verify certificates or applications. Fees, eligibility, documents and deadlines can change; confirm them with the official authority before applying.
